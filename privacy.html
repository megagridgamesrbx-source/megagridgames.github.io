Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

MegaGridGames explains here how information is handled when you visit this website.

Current Website

This website currently provides an informational page. Its Roblox connection button is a preview and does not authenticate users or collect Roblox passwords.

Information and Third Parties

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical information when visitors access hosted pages. Please consult GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Future Account Connections

If Roblox OAuth account connections are introduced, this policy will be updated before launch to explain the information accessed, its purpose, retention, and deletion.

Security

Never enter your Roblox password on this website. Account authorization should take place through Roblox's official authorization page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the website operator through an official MegaGridGames community channel.
